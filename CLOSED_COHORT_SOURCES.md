These collectors capture actual original source facts for the finite Board and
online Arkoor cohort. They do not emit `financial_operating_readiness`, declare
treasury membership, dispatch wallets, or waive any financial gate.

`verify_bootstrap_zero_assets(store, config, chain, actor, action_id,
bootstrap_declaration=value)` is called by `identity_bootstrap.verify_created`
after its original fresh-private-root, sole-writer and exact artifact/config
checks. It loads the immutable declaration, completed zero-fee create and unique
original driver response itself. The pinned SDK uses one external BIP84 keychain;
no internal keychain is invented. A native graph must be complete and empty.
Independent Core validates its canonical public ranged descriptor and scripts,
scans indexes 0..19 in the UTXO set at the exact synchronized tip, and checks every
transaction in a stable mempool of at most 64 transactions for those scripts.
An overrun, source error, asset, incomplete graph or tip/mempool change defers.
Fresh-root provenance justifies this finite derivation window; this API cannot
prove arbitrary restored wallets empty. Its original scoped receipt is
`identity_bootstrap_zero_assets_verified`. It never changes financial admission.

`collect_closed_source_frames(store, config, chain, wallet_actions={...})` takes
exactly four actor/action identities: the bank and three customers, already
observed through the Engine. Each completed create/snapshot is matched to its
unique original `driver_response`, exact result, event hash and digest. Aggregate
native frames are bounded by 2 MiB and 2,000 rows. It invokes only the fixed
`closed_frontier` and `financial_readiness` bridge operations and captures their
original responses through the existing bounded traced process helper. The
collection reports independent tip agreement and explicit remaining monetary
gaps; it never converts a native graph's complete flag to exhaustive assets.
An externally managed bank requires a separately authenticated actual source;
it cannot be silently omitted or substituted by a customer.
The optional `bank_source_sequence` accepts the original event produced by
`funding_bank.collect_funding_bank_source`, kind `treasury_bank_original_source`,
and exactly three customer actions instead. Its exact named Core wallet,
canonical public descriptors, complete source projection, read-only status and
independent tip must match; the original sequence/hash and frame are retained.
